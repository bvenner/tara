# Wright (2005) — The Social Architecture of Capitalism

**Citation:** Wright, I. (2005). The social architecture of capitalism. *Physica A*, 346(3–4), 589–620. DOI: 10.1016/j.physa.2004.08.006. arXiv: cond-mat/0401053.

**Core content:** Agent-based model of the worker–capitalist social relation. Self-organizes into a dynamic equilibrium whose statistical properties match a broad range of empirical regularities: power-law firm size, Laplace growth rates, lognormal–Pareto income, gamma-like firm profit-rate distribution (as predicted by Farjoun–Machover), business-cycle fluctuations. Value categories emerge from simulation rather than being imposed.

**Relevance to program:** The template for Track C's agent-based experiments. The program's hyperbolic model makes *testable predictions for such simulations*: finite-speed propagation of sectoral shocks, a measurable relaxation spectrum, and telegraph-type (oscillatory, front-like) rather than purely diffusive correlation decay. Wright-style ABMs provide the laboratory in which the continuum theory's relaxation times can be measured and the hyperbolic-vs-parabolic discrimination performed.
