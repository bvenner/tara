# Ruggeri & Strumia (1981) — Main Field and Convex Covariant Density

**Citation:** Ruggeri, T., & Strumia, A. (1981). Main field and convex covariant density for quasi-linear hyperbolic systems. *Annales de l'Institut Henri Poincaré, Section A*, 34(1), 65–84.

**Core content:** The structural theorem of RET. For a first-order quasi-linear system of balance laws compatible with a convex entropy extension (an additional scalar balance law with entropy density concave in the state variables), there exists a privileged choice of field variables — the **main field**, identified with the Lagrange multipliers of the entropy inequality (Liu's procedure, I-S. Liu 1972) — in which the system becomes **symmetric hyperbolic**. Consequences: real finite characteristic speeds; local well-posedness of the Cauchy problem (existence, uniqueness, continuous dependence on data) by Friedrichs–Lax theory.

**Context:** Godunov (1961) first symmetrized Euler fluids via a convex extension; Friedrichs & Lax (1971) generalized; Ruggeri–Strumia showed the main-field variables reveal the symmetry of the *physically motivated* system itself. Boillat & Ruggeri (1997, ARMA 137:305–320) extended to principal subsystems and subcharacteristic conditions.

**Relevance to program:** The existence proof template for H2. If a convex "entropy" can be constructed for the value-price system (a concave functional of the extended state — prices, profit rates, capital fluxes), then the main-field change of variables makes the system symmetric hyperbolic, delivering well-posedness *for free*. The program's entropy-search problem is thus well-posed as a mathematical task: find the convex extension or prove non-existence for given closures.
