# Cottrell, Cockshott, Michaelson, Wright & Yakovenko (2009) — Classical Econophysics

**Citation:** Cottrell, A. F., Cockshott, W. P., Michaelson, G. J., Wright, I. P., & Yakovenko, V. M. (2009). *Classical Econophysics*. London: Routledge. ISBN 9780415696463.

**Core content:** Monograph applying econophysics and computational methods to classical political economy: production, exchange, distribution, finance. The Ricardian labor theory of value as an emergent property in agent-based models; Farjoun–Machover price theory; class and profit-rate distributions from simulation; money and credit. Yakovenko's contribution connects to the statistical mechanics of money (Drăgulescu & Yakovenko 2000: kinetic exchange models with Gibbs-like money distributions).

**Relevance to program:** Defines the field this program situates itself in ("Marxian econophysics" as a branch of classical econophysics). Provides the ABM methodology for Track C and the statistical-equilibrium framing (Foley's work is adjacent) that the deterministic balance-law core must be reconciled with. Key gap it leaves open: the dynamics in these models are discrete-time simulations; the continuum (PDE/balance-law) description of the same phenomena — and its hyperbolic structure — is exactly what this program adds.
