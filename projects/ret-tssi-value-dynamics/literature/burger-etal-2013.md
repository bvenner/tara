# Burger, Caffarelli, Markowich & Wolfram (2013) — A Boltzmann-Type Price Formation Model

**Citation:** Burger, M., Caffarelli, L., Markowich, P. A., & Wolfram, M.-T. (2013). On a Boltzmann-type price formation model. *Proceedings of the Royal Society A*, 469, 20130126. DOI: 10.1098/rspa.2013.0126. (Builds on Lasry & Lions 2007 parabolic free-boundary price-formation model.)

**Core content:** Presents a kinetic (Boltzmann-type) model of price formation in a market with buyers/sellers, motivated by the parabolic Lasry–Lions free-boundary equation. Proves convergence of the kinetic model's solutions to the parabolic model's solutions **as the transaction rate tends to infinity** — a rigorous hydrodynamic/relaxation limit in an economic setting. Analyzes the initial layer on the fast time scale.

**Relevance to program:** The key existence proof that relaxation-limit methodology is *already operative* in mathematical economics, at the price-formation level. The program extends the same logic one level up: from transaction-level price formation to system-level value-price equalization. Also a citation shield for the applied-math audience: singular limits from kinetic/hyperbolic to parabolic descriptions are an accepted tool in this literature. Methodological template for how to state and prove H4 rigorously (scaling, initial layers, convergence rates).
