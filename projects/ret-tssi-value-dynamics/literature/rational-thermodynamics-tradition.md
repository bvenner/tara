# The Rational Thermodynamics Tradition — Coleman–Noll (1963), Coleman (1964), Truesdell (1984), Gurtin–Williams (1967), Müller (1967), Liu (1972)

**Citations:**
- Coleman, B. D., & Noll, W. (1963). The thermodynamics of elastic materials with heat conduction and viscosity. *Archive for Rational Mechanics and Analysis*, 13, 167–178. DOI: 10.1007/BF01262690
- Coleman, B. D. (1964). Thermodynamics of materials with memory. *ARMA*, 17, 1–46.
- Gurtin, M. E., & Williams, W. O. (1967). An axiomatic foundation for continuum thermodynamics. *ARMA*, 26, 83–117. DOI: 10.1007/BF00285676
- Müller, I. (1967). On the entropy inequality. *ARMA*, 26, 118–141.
- Liu, I.-S. (1972). Method of Lagrange multipliers for exploitation of the entropy principle. *ARMA*, 46, 131–148.
- Truesdell, C. (1984). *Rational Thermodynamics* (2nd ed.). New York: Springer.
- Day, W. A. (1972). *The Thermodynamics of Simple Materials with Fading Memory* (Springer Tracts in Natural Philosophy 22).

**Core content:** The "rational" reconstruction of thermodynamics within continuum mechanics. Coleman–Noll procedure: the Clausius–Duhem inequality must hold for *all* processes compatible with the balance laws (supplies regarded as arbitrarily assignable) — thereby restricting admissible constitutive relations. Coleman's materials-with-memory theory: constitutive quantities are functionals of the *history* of deformation/temperature, with a fading-memory topology — temporal non-locality tamed by smoothness postulates. The critical fork: Müller (1967) generalized the entropy flux beyond q/θ (extra entropy flux k); Liu (1972) replaced the supply argument with Lagrange multipliers enforcing the balance laws as constraints. These two moves define the divergence between Truesdell-school rational thermodynamics and the Müller–Liu–Ruggeri line that became RET. Recent comparative work (e.g., Cimmelli–Sellitto–Triani generalized procedures) notes subtleties: heat-flux relaxation alone does not guarantee hyperbolicity.

**Relevance to program:** Three uses. (1) The fading-memory/memory-functional formalism is the *alternative* temporalization of constitutive relations — in the economic analogy it corresponds to path-dependent (integral) closures rather than state-space (differential) extensions; Track A should justify why the Cattaneo/state-space route is preferred for value dynamics (Markovianization, well-posedness, identifiable relaxation times). (2) The Müller–Liu vs Coleman–Noll "two procedures" problem is Track B2's subject. (3) The entropy-principle-as-constitutive-selection-rule is the methodology the program applies to economic closures — directly answering the Mohun–Veneziani underdetermination charge.
