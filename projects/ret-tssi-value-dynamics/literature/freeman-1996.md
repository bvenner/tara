# Freeman (1996) — Price, Value and Profit: A Continuous, General Treatment

**Citation:** Freeman, A. (1996). Price, value and profit — a continuous, general treatment. In A. Freeman & G. Carchedi (Eds.), *Marx and Non-Equilibrium Economics*. Cheltenham: Edward Elgar. (Chapter 13; also MPRA Paper 1290, https://mpra.ub.uni-muenchen.de/1290/)

**Core claim:** Provides a general mathematical specification of a non-equilibrium interpretation of Marx's theory of value in *continuous time*. Refutes the Okishio theorem and dissolves the transformation problem within the TSSI framework.

**Key structure:**
- Passes from discrete/serial-time (difference-equation, sequential) formalizations to continuous-time (differential-equation, temporal) formalizations of the conservation of value.
- Treats the MELT as a time-varying field coupling monetary magnitudes to labor-time magnitudes.
- Shows the conservation of value holds identically along the trajectory, not just at a fixed point.

**Relevance to program:** This is the natural mathematical substrate for Track A: the value-price dynamics are already differential equations here, so the Cattaneo-type extension (promoting fluxes to state variables) can be built directly on this formulation rather than on the discrete Kliman–McGlone form. The program's "balance-law" rewriting starts from Freeman's continuous-time conservation equations.
