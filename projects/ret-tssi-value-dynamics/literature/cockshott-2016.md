# Cockshott (2016) — Marxian Reproduction Prices Versus Prices of Production: Probability and Convergence

**Citation:** Cockshott, W. P. (2016). *Marxian Reproduction Prices Versus Prices of Production: Probability and Convergence*. Working Paper, University of Glasgow. ePrints: https://eprints.gla.ac.uk/116697/

**Core content:** Identifies a third price theory implicit in Marx's Volume II reproduction schemes (relative sectoral prices under simple reproduction). Uses random-matrix techniques to put a measure on the configuration space of prices of production vs. labor values. Simulates reproduction schemes over time under capital movement assumptions: **some systems dynamically converge on prices of production and some fail to converge**.

**Relevance to program:** The most direct evidence for H3's premise: convergence to prices of production is conditional, not generic. The program's SK-type coupling condition promises a *criterion* discriminating the convergent from non-convergent cases — turning Cockshott's simulation finding into a theorem-level diagnostic. Also methodologically important for Track C: shows random-ensemble + simulation methodology applied directly to the transformation/gravitation question.
