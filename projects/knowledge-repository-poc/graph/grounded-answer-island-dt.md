# Grounded answer (opencode arm, M7 comparison)

## Question

Develop an island digital twin: metabolic flow models on a realistic 3D
model of an island, for participatory sustainability planning

## Answer

Compose the twin as four tightly-coupled legs. Flow layer: island
socio-metabolic MFA/MSA accounting. Substrate: semantic 3D island model in
CityGML 3.0 carrying metabolic flows through the Energy ADE v3.0 Resources
module. Container: a decoupled digital twin per Batty, enriched socially and
aligned to the EU LDT standards stack. Process: PPGIS/PSS methods run
through the geodesign iteration. The novel publishable coupling is
metabolism + 3D-standard; no integrated island twin in that sense is
published (closest analogues: Inishmore peer-to-peer energy twin, Culatra
grid twin, ARGOS Kefalonia).
