# The question list — v1

*Workflow D1: the question list is **joint and reciprocal** — prepared by the researcher (language questions from the glossary work) together with the lead developer and the interviewer (fieldwork questions from T1/T2); **roughly half the meeting's substance is our questions to them**. This v1 is the prepared half plus the accounting side (questions they may ask us, with what we can answer honestly today). The fieldwork half merges in at M−10→M−5 (A3). Brief and plain-language throughout (QA presentation convention, 2026-11-03).*

## 1. Our questions to the circle (draft v1)

**About money and tools (T1 — the budget question):**
1. What do you pay today for the software and services your back office actually runs on — and is any of it a subscription you'd happily drop?
2. The records say nothing about price; what does a good research or data tool have to cost to be credible here?
3. What did you try and stop using — and why?

**About impact language and measurement (T5 — through the glossary work):**
4. When a portfolio company reports "impact," what do you actually do with the number?
5. Which pieces of the current measure would you keep, which would you drop, and — plainly — **who chose them, and when do you get to revise them?**
6. If your investees could ship their data in one shared format instead of filling in each funder's form, would you use it? What would you need changed?

**About the shared back office (their own framing at the table):**
7. You named a shared back office at the first meeting and moved on. What would it take to make it real — and what stops it today?
8. What would a cross-fund research person need in the first month to be useful to *all* of you?

**About being a good participant (the exchange itself):**
9. What would make this circle worth your time six months from now?
10. Whose interests are affected by these investments that are not represented in this room — and who *should* carry their voice, in your view? *(asked once, honestly — workflow D3; plain English; no movement vocabulary)*

## 2. What we expect to be asked, and our prepared accounts

| Likely question | Our honest account today |
|---|---|
| "What is this tool in a sentence?" | A server (Fortress-based) that small funds and cooperatives can self-host, with the shared research and reporting layer added on top — we demo what exists and propose the rest. *(drawn from the locked framing; demo C3 gates the specifics)* |
| "What's the business model?" | A subscription from funds for the B2B/research layer — priced against what you tell us you pay (that's question 1). We publish assumptions and changes to them. |
| "Why open source? Why would you not be acquired?" | The core remains open (Fortress is); the add-on layer is the support business. A cooperative developer (proletariat.tech) is the steward — our obligation runs to the network, and the site states what we don't know. |
| "What is 'measurable impact' to you?" | Multidimensional by default; the field's five-dimension norm is where we start, and any single score we ever produce will carry the revision question — who chooses it, and when can the circle change it. *(glossary; standing-question apparatus B3)* |
| "What don't you know?" | Whether funds will pay at the level that sustains the work; what "measurable" survives an investment committee; what the affected public of portfolio investments should measure that neither side yet tracks. The site's *What we don't know* section is the standing list. |
| "Who are you?" | PT's lead developer (they build); the research and the language work (this document, the site) are the support half. No entity names; the audience is invited to name the things we build — working names are placeholders. |
