## 1. Scope

What the options memo must answer — as options with trade-offs, never a recommendation:

1. A model of the federation's top-down capital direction as a decision process across the three impact domains (OR/control framing), with the modeling platform as the decision instrument.
2. Surplus pooling, solidarity-fund, and cross-subsidy designs grounded in cooperative game theory and network science.
3. A business process model of the co-op lifecycle (formation → funding → governance onboarding → monitoring → scaling/replication → exit/legacy), with the impact domains entering as decision variables.
4. The anti-degeneration answer: how allocation resists the classic labor-managed-firm pathologies (underinvestment, drift back to capitalist form).
5. Research proposals: the open problems the R&D program should own.

Out of scope: final allocation rules; real numbers (illustrative only); the governance questions owned by B1 (referenced where coupled).

*(v2) Terminology adopted throughout (M3 L327):* **internal returns** (inside the network's accounting) vs **external returns** (owed to capital outside it) — external returns are minimized, always; internal returns are what the network compounds.

## 2. Research questions

- **RQ1.** How should capital direction be modeled as a multi-objective, multi-timescale decision process (financial return, planetary, social), and what does "rapidly rising internal IRR" mean as a control objective rather than a slogan? *(v2: the transcript answer — internal IRR must exceed external IRR so external capital can be bought out and replaced by internal capital (M3 L415–419) — is the hypothesis to test.)*
- **RQ2.** How should solidarity funds, internal capital accounts, and cross-subsidy be structured so the federation is stable in the cooperative-game sense (core) and legible as fair?
- **RQ3.** What is the lifecycle process model, and where do the three impact domains gate funding decisions?
- **RQ4.** How does the mechanism answer the Ward–Domar–Vanek degeneration canon and the underinvestment critique — and what does empirical employee-ownership performance show?
- **RQ5.** How do network effects (data compounding, inter-cooperation, industrial-symbiosis-style flows) change the allocation calculus?
- *(v2)* **RQ6 (M3 L403, 435 — the flagship).** How are returns *drawn from the whole system* ("the circulatory system") rather than extracted organ-by-organ — and how much can be withdrawn without killing the organism?
