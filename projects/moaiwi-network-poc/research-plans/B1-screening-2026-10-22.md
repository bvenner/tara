## Screening decisions of note

- The federation-architecture literature is **thin**: only seven P0 items, and two are conference/journal first insights. This is itself a finding — WP1's comparison matrix will be assembled as much from case literature (Mondragon, Emilia-Romagna, Evergreen) as from federation-specific studies.
- The **agricultural co-op federation** literature (France, India, Brazil, Kenya) dominates the available federation evidence. Useful for mechanism, weak for worker-coop federations — note the transfer risk explicitly in the memo.
- **Tax/holding-structure material exists mostly as tax-law commentary** (Netherlands intermediate holding) rather than as co-op design literature, which supports keeping tax as counsel-flagged research questions rather than a deck claim.
- Bank-holding-company topology is off-topic for B1 but a plausible **B4** seed (higher-order structure of nested entities) — handed to B4 rather than dropped.
