Concepts

The words in use, their definitions credited to their sources, and what using each word commits you to.

Impact
"a change in outcomes for people and the planet" (Common Impact Data Standard introductory guide).
The five dimensions of impact
what, who, how much, contribution, risk ( + how) — the Impact Management Norms, developed with 2,000+ practitioners, housed at Impact Frontiers; represented in the Common Impact Data Standard.
The Common Impact Data Standard
A free, open way to package an organization's impact model and data so it can move between software without re-mapping ("one shared container, no prescribed indicators"). Required reporting infrastructure for Canada's Social Finance Fund participants.
Solidarity economy
"a movement and a system built on cooperation, participatory democracy, equity, sustainability, pluralism, community self-determination, and internationalism" (U.S. Solidarity Economy Network).

What we don't know

  • Whether funds will pay for this at the level that sustains the work — the question we came to ask.
  • What "measurable" survives a real investment committee.
  • Which impact-data tools people here actually run, and what they cost.
  • Whether one shared data format would actually save anyone time yet (adoption of data standards is early; we checked — see the concepts section).
  • Whose interests the system affects that neither of us currently measures.

The revision question travels with every metric we ever show: who chose this, and when does the collectivity get to revise it?
