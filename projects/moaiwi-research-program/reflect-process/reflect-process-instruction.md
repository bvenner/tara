## Terminology (binding for new text)

- **meta-** means **self-application**. *meta-data = data about data; meta-plan = a plan for developing plans; meta-process = a process applied to processes — one that develops processes.* Use "meta-" only when X operates on X.
- The four (**Research, Develop, Deploy, Reflect**) are **"the four processes"** in new text. Historical documents (`meta-plan-v3.md`, `research-topics-v3.md`) call them "the four meta-processes"; that loose usage is inherited, not binding — harmonization rides with the pending C1–C7 revision batch.
- A Reflect session applied to the four processes or to Reflect itself is a **meta-process** (it develops processes). The meta-process topic cluster: **T13, T14, T15, T16, T17, T18** (T16 included by Brad, 2026-10-27 — its method is meta even though its terminal-object content is substantive).

## What a Reflect process is

A structured, recorded dialogue (QA sessions) between Brad and an agent that revises the program's own objects: terminal object, conventions, the four processes, this instruction. Output shape: a **meta-plan**, a revision of one, **or a revision of the research agenda (`research-topics-v3.md`) and of the program's conventions** (2026-10-27: the meta-process review's product is RT revisions — specifications of the four processes were ruled premature). Conflicts are **noted, not silently resolved**.

Structural doctrine: the **X → F(X) shape** — the Reflect realization produces the specification of the processes, including its own — and **incompleteness**: every specification is a procedural realization and stays revisable. Hence this instruction carries its own revision procedure.

## Session mechanics

1. **QA discipline** (adopted from QA session 1, 2026-10-25; the canonical detail lives in `qa-2026-10-25-terminal-object.md` in this directory): numbered questions and answers; **no document changes and no non-Q&A output until a jointly declared stopping point**; lookups only per QA session 1's option (a).
2. **Chunked deliberation** (2026-10-27): the agent asks in numbered batches; Brad answers subsets; **the agent responds to each response (analysis, consequences, revised positions) before sending the next batch**. One topic per exchange where possible.
3. **Recording.** Each session gets `qa-YYYY-MM-DD-<topic>.md` in **this directory** — the memorial archive future reflect processes inspect (precedents: `qa-2026-10-25-terminal-object.md`, `qa-2026-10-27-meta-processes.md`). Every change agreed but not yet applied goes to that record's **deferred-changes ledger** (#1, #2, …), applied as a batch at a stopping point. Referenced documents get short names per session (precedent: **Castor**, **Paideia**).
4. **Outputs.** Meta-plan-shaped: a meta-plan, a revision batch, or a convention decision. Emerging research questions are recorded in `research-topics-v3.md` (precedent: T13's extension; T18's creation).
