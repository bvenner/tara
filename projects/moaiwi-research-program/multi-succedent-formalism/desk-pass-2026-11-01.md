# Desk pass — the multi-succedent formalism question (2026-11-01)

*Bounded desk pass commissioned by Brad (2026-11-01) before the formalism work proceeds. ~8 search queries, no deep reads. The question: is there a compositional diagrammatic formalism covering (i) hypergraph KBs, (ii) relational structures, (iii) possibly NMMS material bases — one premise configuration yielding **multiple succedents** as the common feature? Success criterion: three translations + loss profiles. Sources are leads verified at abstract level; nothing below is a reading of full texts.*

## Verdict per lead

1. **Hypergraph ↔ string diagram: established machinery.** String Diagram Rewrite Theory I–III (Bonchi–Gadducci–Kissinger–Sobocinski–Zanasi; arXiv:2012.01847, 2104.14686, 2109.06049; MSCS 2022): string diagrams correspond to **monogamous acyclic (labelled) hypergraphs**; rewriting is double-pushout with interfaces (DPOI); sound and complete with Frobenius; convex DPO for arbitrary symmetric monoidal theories; **confluence decidable** for DPOI. Syntax side of (i) is basically solved; the *logic* is what's missing.
2. **Relational ↔ categorical KR: established.** Spivak–Kent, *Ologs: A Categorical Framework for KR* (PLoS ONE 2012): ologs ≈ categorical database schemas in natural language; RDF/OWL via Grothendieck construction. **Functorial data migration** (Spivak; Spivak–Wisnesky, *Relational Foundations for FDM*, arXiv:1212.5303; FQL): schema morphisms induce Δ/Σ/Π migration functors — literally **translation-with-loss machinery** for the success criterion.
3. **Proof structure ↔ graphs: established, and central.** Hughes, *Proofs without syntax* (Annals 2006): combinatorial proofs = **graph homomorphisms** h: C → G(φ). *Logic without syntax* (arXiv:math/0504065): classical (multi-conclusion) logic in constructions over **Rel**. Additive/multi-succedent structure: Heijltjes–Hughes–Straßburger additive proof nets (FSCD 2019); conflict nets for MALL (LICS 2016); **Petri-net connection** (Heijltjes–Hughes, LICS 2015); *Normalization without syntax* (FSCD 2022).
4. **Brown's NMMS reconstruction** (arXiv:2605.24796v2, Oct 2026; ACT 2026 → EPTCS): NMMS semantics via universal constructions; connectives from adjunctions; references lean on **Barr's \*-autonomous categories and linear logic**. His future work = our question ("a formal statement relating traditional categorical logic and logical expressivism"). ROLE.jl = "a library for implication space semantics" (cited 2026). **Ambient-setting hypothesis: \*-autonomous/linear** (the left/right tensor asymmetry), additive structure carrying the multi-succedent side.
5. **The NMMS-conservativity finding** (Hlobil–Brandom, *Reasons for Logic, Logic for Reasons* (Routledge 2025) per Horty's NDPR review): **what is non-monotonic is the material base of sequents**, not the logic; NMMS is conservative over its base; **Cut is not admissible**. Hlobil, *Choosing your nonmonotonic logic: a shopper's guide* (2018) = evaluation lead.
6. **The T17 bridge:** Libkind–Myers, *Towards a double operadic theory of systems* (arXiv:2505.18329) — DOTS (symmetric monoidal loose right modules over symmetric monoidal double categories); a section explicitly relates **operadic systems theories and process theories** (KSW97 line; Courser's thesis). Process theories (SMCs with in/out) ⊂ categorical systems theory. Also: *Colored Petri Nets are Monoidal Double Functors* (arXiv:2510.01946); DCST "Representable Behaviour" (Topos Colloquium 2025-03-06).
7. **Ologs ↔ strings/DB detail:** Spivak–Wisnesky FQL; *Toward formalizing ologs* (arXiv:1503.08326, updated 2026).

## The gap

**Nobody does the three-way translation with NMMS in it.** Two of the three legs (hypergraph↔strings; Rel↔KR) are established; the proof leg is established over Rel for classical/multi-succedent logic. The unknown is a diagrammatic doctrine carrying NMMS's non-monotonic multi-succedent structure — plausibly in the \*-autonomous/linear ambient setting, with the *material-base revision discipline* (D3 of the QA record) as the semantics that must survive translation.
