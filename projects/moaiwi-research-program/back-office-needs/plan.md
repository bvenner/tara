# T2 plan — back-office needs and incumbent tools of impact-investor clients

*Clean-sheet plan, 2026-10-27, keyed to the research question (RT.T2, `research-topics-v3.md`). Formed by merging the AC.A4 software-survey requirements into the topic (Brad, 2026-10-27) — the R.1 landscape deliverable is an **interim slice** of this project, not its scope. No gates (meta-plan §4.1). Shares the fieldwork surface with T1.*

## Question

What do these funds' back offices actually do all day? What tools do they use (accounting, portfolio tracking, impact reporting, research)? Where do they complain? What would make them switch or add a subscription? (Extension carried from AC.A4: map what the market *offers* against what the funds actually *run* — the gap = the wedge.)

## Decisions informed

- Feature prioritization for the Fortress B2B addition.
- The demo's content (Fortress demo + research-stack proposal).
- Whether any incumbent is beatable on reliability *and* price.

## Work packages

- **WP1 — desk incumbent-landscape (started 2026-10-27).** Categories: fund accounting / portfolio monitoring (GP-side vs. LP-side), CRM/origination, impact measurement & reporting, research tooling, and the spreadsheet layer underneath. Sources: vendor sites, G2/Capterra, job postings, fund tech-stack traces. **Interim landscape slice for R.1 by M−5** (shared with T1 — see `landscape-pass1-2026-10-27.md`).
- **WP2 — interview instrument.** "Listen to them complain" questions seeded by WP1 (what they run, what they pay, what breaks, what they evaluated and rejected, what's still in spreadsheets). Joint with Nic and Rachel (action-circle workflow D1).
- **WP3 — fieldwork.** Same window and coordination as T1 (Nic/Rachel; M1 interviews + circle meetings).
- **WP4 — synthesis.** Jobs-to-be-done map; complaint inventory; switching-criteria matrix (reliability, price, migration cost).

## Expected outputs

- Incumbent-tools landscape note (R.1 slice; feeds the site's "what exists vs. what is proposed" distinctions).
- Complaint inventory (verbatim where possible).
- Switching-criteria matrix; feature-prioritization input.

## Resources and access

- Web research as above (bot walls limit coverage; interviews supply ground truth). ~$20 LLM budget.
- Access: the action-circle meeting(s), M1 interviews, USSEN-adjacent contacts.

## What counts as an answer / closure

- *Answer:* a defensible map of what the comparable funds' back offices actually run, where the complaints cluster, and what would make them switch or add a subscription.
- *Closure:* interview saturation — new conversations stop yielding new tools, complaints, or switching criteria.

## Where results are recorded

- Notes in this directory (starting with `landscape-pass1-2026-10-27.md`); the RT.T2 entry updated as findings land; the landscape slice feeds the action-circle workflow (B2/C1).
