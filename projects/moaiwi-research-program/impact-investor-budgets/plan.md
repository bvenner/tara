# T1 plan — what impact-investor funds actually pay for software and research support

*Clean-sheet plan, 2026-10-27, keyed to the research question (RT.T1, `research-topics-v3.md`). Formed by merging the AC.A4 software-survey requirements into the topic (Brad, 2026-10-27) — the R.1 landscape deliverable is an **interim slice** of this project, not its scope. No gates (meta-plan §4.1).*

## Question

Per client per year: what is the software/services budget of the action-circle funds and comparable USSEN-adjacent funds? Do they buy software, or services, or nothing? (Extension carried from AC.A4: what does the market *offer* and at what price — the vendor landscape and pricing models — since the gap between offered and used is the wedge.)

## Decisions informed

- The revenue arithmetic ($50k ÷ ~20 = ~$2.5k/client/yr vs. 5–10 at $5k–10k) — meta-plan §3.1's "risk noted": *M1 must establish this*.
- The choice between the (a) replace-software-spend and (b) grow-the-fund halves of the value logic.
- Pricing in M2.

## Work packages

- **WP1 — desk market scan (started 2026-10-27).** Vendor landscape + pricing: impact-investing-specific platforms, private-capital portfolio monitoring, fund accounting, and the general back-office stack; published pricing where it exists (mostly quote-based — itself a finding). **Interim landscape slice for R.1 by M−5** (shared with T2 — see `../back-office-needs/landscape-pass1-2026-10-27.md`).
- **WP2 — interview instrument.** Draft questions seeded by WP1 (budgets, what they pay, renewal pain, research support spend — including the MOAIWI-4 "budget for a person" remark). Joint with Nic and Rachel per the action-circle workflow's D1 (the question list is reciprocal).
- **WP3 — fieldwork.** Nic (and maybe Rachel) coordinate; window-sensitive (the M1 interviews and circle meetings; degrades if asked late). Brad proceeds desk-first ahead of fieldwork (Brad, 2026-10-27).
- **WP4 — synthesis.** Per-client-per-year budget ranges with evidence; the software vs. services vs. nothing split; feed the revenue arithmetic.

## Expected outputs

- Interim landscape note (R.1 slice; feeds the action-circle site's product language).
- Interview protocol (with the budget/pricing module).
- Findings note(s) in this directory; updated revenue arithmetic.

## Resources and access

- Web research (vendor sites, G2/Capterra, job postings, fund tech-stack traces); known coverage limits: vendor bot walls, unpublished pricing. ~$20 LLM budget per Brad's constraint.
- Access: the action-circle meeting(s), M1 interviews, USSEN-adjacent contacts (~20 warm intros per MOAIWI-4).

## What counts as an answer / closure

- *Answer:* per-client-per-year software and services budget ranges, with evidence, and the software-vs-services-vs-nothing split — sufficient to choose between value-logic halves (a)/(b) and to set or bound M2 pricing.
- *Closure:* the arithmetic is no longer a hypothesis (or an explicit, evidenced statement that it cannot be set and why).
