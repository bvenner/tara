We have recently been working on two research topics in the moaiwi-resesearch-program project: developing an "autonomy" program to guide the long-term evolution of the "solidarity economy" at [[democracy-as-regime]] and a second research topic that is still being developed in this reflect process: developing an explicit knowledge base (however named) as a formal output of a research process that could be used in a develop process as sketched in the research topic [[research-topic-v3#T18], titled "neurosymbolic knowledge engineering". Currently, TARA is using an off-the-shelf hypergraph library written in Python as it's explicit knowledge base.  which would hopefully help understand and develop a useful approach to explicit knowledge that would complement LLM's and address the needs of a develop process grounded in a vericoding approach (as realized in KVerus, for example). At the core of this proposed topic is Bradley Allen's Elenchus program, which attempts to base a knowledge engineering process on Robert Brandom's book "Reasons for logic, logic for reasons".

While meditating last night on "giving and taking of breath", I was struck by the similarities between Brandom's and Castoriadis' frameworks. Brandom focuses on the giving and taking of reasons and Castoriadis on paideia as "a school of governing and being governed" [[castoriadis-1997-summary]]. In the ideal socialist future, these are converging processes, as governance is transformed from the "giving and taking of commands" into the "giving and taking of reasons", which correspond to the distinction between "power over" and "power with".
