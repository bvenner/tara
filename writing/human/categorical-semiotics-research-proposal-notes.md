# Why now?

I've been playing around with ideas around categorical semiotics (aka category theory applied to semiotics) for many years. I have a number of more concrete research projects sketched in my [[moaiwi-research-program]], including some high-level work on democracy and autonomous society following Castoriadis. 

The blame lies with a clue dropped in Scott Metzger's Ph.D. thesis [Perice's Semiotic Realism](https://prod-ms-be.lib.mcmaster.ca/server/api/core/bitstreams/a5b76ec7-d7b5-42df-a6c7-d33218e15213/content), which stated that a core feature of Ockam's nominalism was to restrict the analysis of language to syntax and semantics, and that analytic philosophy continued in this tradition.

Part of this tradition seems to be carried on within categorical logic, which places a great emphasis on syntax/semantics dualities as adjoint functors. The particular article I was reading was on (Coln)[https://coln-project.github.io/0001/index.xml], the interesting database/proof assistant being developed by Owen Lynch with perhaps an assist from Martin Kleppman.
