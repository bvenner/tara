The meta-plan intends to begin with the end in mind, taking the value of any such attempt on faith. The meta-plan will be used to inform the development of more specific plans. It will outline the development of a higher-order network of workplace cooperatives that attempts to solve previous attempts to scale cooperative networks. The meta-plan is organized in two dimensions, loosely thought of as time and space. The time dimension will be developed as a series of projects. Projects are composed of sub-projects, which will be called a phase. The space dimension will be developed as ongoing meta-processes, which operate during every phase. The projects are called Moaiwi, Hyphae, and Cooptopia. The meta-processes are called Research, Develop, Deploy, and Reflect.

The first project is the development of a business-to-business product that serves the needs of impact investors that are associated with the (U.S. Solidarity Economy Network)[ussen.org], hereafter USSEN. The project is an organic growth from connections that were made between Nic and several impact investors at the recent (Resist and Build conference that was held in Denver, CO in 2026)[https://resistandbuild.net/den-2026/]. The working name for this product is Moaiwi. The developer is the workplace cooperative "proletariat.tech", hereafter PT. The lead developer for PT is Nic Venner; their pronouns are they/them. PT currently has an alpha-stage business-to-consumer product called Fortress. The source code for Fortress is on this computer at /home/brad/Projects/fortress and on Codeberg at https://codeberg.org/proletariat/fortress. Fortress will serve as the basis for Moaiwi. The goal is to make Fortress sufficiently configurable so that the same code base can be used to specify either a home network server or a business server.

The initial target audience for the Moaiwi project are impact investors in USSEN. The revenue goal is to develop a sufficiently large collection of clients to produce annual revenue of $50,000, which would be sufficient to sustain Nic in the continued development of the meta-plan outlined here. This may require expansion of the target audience to impact investors outside of formal association with USSEN. Impact investors have been selected as the initial target audience due to their alignment with the longer-term goals of the meta-plan while having sufficient economic resources to meet the annual revenue targets. 

In order to meet the revenue target, Moaiwi must produce sufficient value for impact investors that they are willing to pay for the software. This would be done by a combination of replacing existing software expenditures and facilitating the growth of the impact investment funds. This means that Moaiwi must meet or exceed the reliability targets defined by existing cloud-based services while also providing for additional new features that are not currently accessible to impact investors. Some of these potential features are described in the transcripts. Additional features will be identified through user interviews, pilot demonstrations, and literature review.

The second project is the development of technology to facilitate a confederation of workplace cooperatives. For the purposes of this document, the working name for this technology will be Hyphae. Hyphae would allow for greater cooperation between impact investors and cooperative networks. Impact investors would provide much-needed equity capital to cooperative networks, and cooperative networks would provide measurable environmental, social and financial impacts to attract additional impact investors. Hyphae would accomplish this by using advanced modeling and analysis approaches to identify opportunities to develop a workplace cooperative that would provide a good or service to grow the cooperative network. Hyphae would be grounded in a model of the local economy and the cooperative network itself. Hyphae would accomplish this goal by replacing a good or service that is procured in the conventional economy by workplace cooperatives or their members in the network with a good or service provided by a cooperative. The replacement process would need to increase environmental, social, and/or financial value. Hyphae would develop a synergy between investors and cooperators that would be greater than either could achieve alone.

The third project is the development of technology to facilitate the growth of the cooperative economy past the current limits to their growth. Even in places where cooperative networks have a substantial presence, such as the Mondragon Corporation, these networks rarely make up more than 5% of total economic flows. We anticipate that these limits are not due to inherent limitations in the cooperative economy but due to current limitations in productive, organizational and information technology. The working name for this project will be Cooptopia. Because of the dependence of this project on Hyphae, it is premature to develop specific project goals and this should be considered a research-only project for now.

The four meta-processes of Research, Develop, Deploy and Reflect will occur during every phase. These meta-processes always interact. These meta-processes will also be the target of technology development and the ability to successfully meet project goals will depend upon the skill of these meta-processes. At any project stage, these meta-processes must be realized as a collection of interacting agents (human or computational) linked together by exchange of resources over channels. The success of the meta-plan will depend upon the ability to dynamically generate processes that realize the meta-process and are appropriate for the project stage. 

The following paragraph outlines the current realization of each meta-process at the current project stage. The Research meta-process is realized by the TARA project in this repository. This project attempts to combine the implicit knowledge of a research question within an LLM with an explicit knowledge representation developed from primary sources and collected in a hypergraph data model. The Develop meta-process currently uses the programming language Rust to specify a collection of systemd processes linked by a declarative NixOS configuration file. The Rust code is developed by LLMs under supervision by Nic. For the Moaiwi project, the Develop process will be based on the repository at We intend to develop a multi-agent vericoding harness that will use Verus to produce formalized Rust applications. The vericoding harness will be based on existing Verus projects like STARverus or KVerus and will use formalized multi-agent processes as anticipated in the Plumbing language in the project ./projects/knowledge-repository-poc but written in Verus. The Deploy meta-process will ensure that technology developed by PT is reliable not only when first introduced but will be sufficiently flexible to grow and change as the needs of the users change. Since PT has not yet deployed Fortress, this project will serve as the basis for implementing a deployment meta-process that will be the basis for deployment of Moaiwi. We intend to eventually develop some sort of digital twin technology in order to automate the continued relevance and reliability of deployed systems. The Reflect process has not been systematically developed. We intend to use the imagination and creativity of developers and users to imagine a better society and how advanced technology can help bring this about. The Reflect process will transcend the current limitations of technology development where only engineers imagine new products.
