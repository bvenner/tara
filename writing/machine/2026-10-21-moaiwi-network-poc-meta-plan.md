# Meta-plan: moaiwi-network-poc

**Date:** 2026-10-21 · **Revised:** 2026-10-22 (per `writing/machine/2026-10-22-moaiwi-transcripts-review.md`) · **Status:** Phase 0 + Phase A executed 2026-10-21; Gate 1 (framing memo) awaiting Nic review; all seven §5 decisions resolved 2026-10-22 · **Kind:** research-plan (meta-plan: plan to develop a plan)

**Objective:** Within 30 days (assume event by ~Fri 2026-11-20; exact date confirmed at Gate 0), produce and present — Nic presenting — a **brief 5–10 minute overview, including a brief live demo**, of a structurally abstract business plan for **a corporation that is also a federation of worker cooperatives**, to an invite-only hybrid gathering of contacts from Resist & Build Summit Denver 2026. The **deck and website are the primary artifacts**: they must be developed far enough to put in front of interested parties at that meeting — the talk is a pointer to them, not the substance. The sprint is **one combined workstream** (prototype stack + impact-fund interviews + conference pitch), not three sequential ones. The goal of the event is **feedback**, not diligence: feedback (including naming/branding input) feeds the subsequent investor-facing-materials phase.

**Pitch thesis (working, revised 2026-10-22):** The corporation's reason to exist is its integrated **multi-capital modeling capability** — financial + planetary (material, energy, ecosystem) + social (democracy, well-being, community) — which makes two things compatible: (a) **bottom-up** formation of worker cooperatives with real impact stories and (b) **top-down** capital direction that rapidly raises internal rates of return by funding cooperatives. The capability is delivered across three horizons: **short** — the stack prototype (built on Nic's existing mycor.io infrastructure) plus impact-fund interviews; **medium** — a **beta product for deployment to funds**: the financial-analysis/back-office wedge (SaaS ≈$10K/yr or cooperative membership) with shared back office, books and observability; **long** — **federation-wide multi-capital modeling** and the investment vehicle (flow analysis, hole-plugging thesis, equity-not-bonds, internal/external returns). Initial investors are **equity-only**.

## 6. Risks & mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Nic's review bandwidth is the critical path | Compact review packages (≤30 min), staggered gates, defaults-if-silent policy agreed at Gate 0. |
| 30-day compression | Scope discipline: synthetic data, options-not-decisions, corpus ingestion limited to B1–B3 reference needs; demo scope frozen at Gate 4. |
| Audience mis-register (movement wariness of capital) | Framing phase centers economic democracy; capital subordinated; co-optation risk named in the plan, not hidden. |
| Illustrative numbers read as projections | "Illustrative — synthetic federation data" disclaimer on every artifact, slide, and demo. |
| Hybrid tech failure | Static hosting, screenshot fallbacks, PDF handout, offline-capable demo builds. |
| Premature specificity (names, legal form, governance) | Structural abstraction policy (A); options memos never recommend single choices; naming deliberately delegated to the audience. |
| Unnamed-entity confusion in the deck | Fixed role-label glossary in the framing memo, applied everywhere. |
| Three workstreams in one sprint (prototype + interviews + pitch) | One integrated workstream with shared artifacts: interviews feed the wedge spec and the deck; the prototype doubles as D0; deck and site are the only show artifacts; Gate 2b checkpoint on Nov 4. |
| Formal-framing drift toward categorical machinery | Operative language fixed as **higher-order networks (B4)**; cost accounting grounded in existing business processes; categorical material parked as long-horizon seeds only. |
| Short slot (5–10 min) leaves no room for the argument | Deck and site carry the argument; the talk is a pointer plus one brief live demo; rehearsal to a hard time box at Gate 5. |
